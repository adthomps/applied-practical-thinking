CRT World is the live author platform for C. R. Thompson.

## What a visitor can verify

https://crthompson.net/ is the public site, The Worlds of C. R. Thompson. The same Cloudflare app has an editorial admin. A Worker API and a D1 database hold the published content.

## What must not be claimed

The public site is the discovery surface. It is not a claim that every editorial tool is exposed to anonymous visitors.
