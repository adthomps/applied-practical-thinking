APT Health Compass is a working app for reading periodic health results next to the supplements taken that month.

## What a visitor can verify

A signed-in person can review their own imported results and the supplement record for that period. The public site is the app, not a shared dataset.

## What must not be claimed

It does not give medical advice. It does not publish anyone's health records. It does not log workouts, meals, or daily Apple Health sleep.
