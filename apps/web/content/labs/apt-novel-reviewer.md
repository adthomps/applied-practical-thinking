APT Novel Reviewer is a local desktop prototype for personal writing. It imports a manuscript, splits it into chapters, and runs review modes through a local model.

Findings stay on the machine. Later versions can be compared so a note shows up as resolved, still open, or new. It does not rewrite the manuscript in the app.
