## Problem And Audience

A health product is easy to overclaim. The audience needs to see that a working review exists, and that other people's results are not part of the public site.

## Intended Outcome

Let a signed-in person compare periodic results with the supplements taken that month, and read a practical next step from that comparison.

## Thinking And Decision

Health records stay with the account that imported them. The public pages describe the product. They do not display a sample patient's data as if it were a research set.

Tradeoff: a stranger cannot click through someone else's results. The proof is the product boundary, not a public dashboard of health data.

## What Was Left Out

Medical advice, published health records, workout logs, meal logs, and daily Apple Health sleep feeds.
